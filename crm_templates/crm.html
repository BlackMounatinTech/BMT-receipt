Sales Call

1. Opening

“Hey, how’s it going? Good. Let’s jump right into this and waste no time.

I’m going to ask you a bunch of questions to see if I can help you with your advertising. I’m quite sure I can because of [the specific things in my notes about your company and ads].

Then I’ll explain how the advertising works, and we’ll talk about the contract at the end. Does that sound fair?”

2. Questions

Questions to be added.

3. Explain the advertising

Explain how our advertising works: choose the projects they want more of, use their completed work to create the ads, and run Facebook and Instagram campaigns that bring enquiries through a contact form.

Explain how those enquiries reach their team and how they turn a qualified enquiry into a quoted job.

4. Questions & back and forth

Find out where they stand on the offer. Ask questions, hear their concerns, and talk through what they want to change about their advertising.

Help them decide whether the approach makes sense for their company and whether they want to move forward.

5. The close

  1. Confirm the package, deliverables, ad budget, and payment option.
  2. Send the contract, walk through it together, and collect the signed agreement.
  3. Send the selected Stripe link and confirm payment has been received.
  4. Book a 20-minute onboarding call. Confirm the date, time, and time zone.
  5. Send the transaction summary and onboarding email below, with the meeting details filled in.
