Candidate follow-up

Closer questionnaire.

Copy the text below into an email or message. The candidate can type answers under each question and reply directly to you.

On your phone, press and hold the text to select and copy it. This is a copy-and-paste questionnaire, not an online submission form.

BMT CLOSER FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONNAIRE Thanks for speaking with me. Please reply with your answers beneath each question. Short, specific answers are great. There’s space at the bottom for your questions too. 1. YOUR DETAILS Name: Email: Phone: Location and time zone: 2. RELEVANT EXPERIENCE How many years have you been closing? What have you personally sold, at what prices, and to which buyers? What parts of the sales process did you own? Answer: 3. YOUR RESULTS AND LEAD SOURCE For one recent role, state the period, calls booked, calls attended and customers who paid. Where did the leads come from? How warm were they, and what did they know before the call? If you don’t have exact numbers, label estimates clearly. Answer: 4. QUALIFICATION A contractor joins a call knowing little about BMT. What are the first questions you would ask to establish whether they’re a fit? What would make you decide not to sell to them? Answer: 5. OBJECTION SCENARIO A contractor says: “We get our work through referrals. Why would I spend $3,000 on this?” Write what you would actually say next and what you would ask before recommending anything. Answer: 6. FOLLOW-UP SCENARIO The call ends with: “Send me something. I need to think.” What would you send, what would your next steps be, and how would you decide when to stop following up? Answer: 7. WORKING TOGETHER What exact days and hours can you reliably take calls? Include your time zone, other commitments and earliest start date. How would you track calls and follow-ups, and communicate lead-quality concerns or ask for help? Answer: 8. FEEDBACK AND LONG-TERM INTEREST Give one example of feedback you applied to improve your selling. What interests you about working with BMT, and what would you want from the role over the next 6–12 months? Answer: 9. EVIDENCE AND EXPECTATIONS Can you provide a previous manager/client reference or a relevant sales recording you have permission to share? Do not send confidential customer information. What commission arrangement and payout timing are you looking for? Answer: 10. YOUR QUESTIONS FOR ME What would you like to know about the packages, lead quality, call volume, fulfilment, compensation or expectations before deciding whether this is right for you? Questions: Thanks, Michael Black Mountain Technologies
