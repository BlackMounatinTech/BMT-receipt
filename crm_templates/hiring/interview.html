Michael’s interview guide

Closer interview.

Seven questions, a five-minute role-play, then next steps. Allow 15–20 minutes.

“Thanks for making time. I handle outreach and book meetings for BMT. I’m looking for someone to run the sales calls, qualify the contractor, close the right package and follow up. I’ll ask a few questions, then we’ll do a quick role-play.”

01 · Ask these seven questions

Get to know their experience.

  1. “What have you personally sold, at what prices, and to whom?”
  2. “What were your actual results, and how warm were the leads?”

    How many attended calls became paying customers? Did the leads come from setters, ads or cold outreach?

  3. “How do you qualify someone before pitching?”

    What if they join the call knowing very little about us?

  4. “How do you handle ‘That’s too expensive’ or ‘I need to think about it’?”

    Ask them to demonstrate what they would say.

  5. “What do you do when someone doesn’t close on the call?”

    Ask how they follow up and track the next step.

  6. “What hours can you work, in which time zone, and when could you start?”
  7. “What interests you about BMT, and are you comfortable with the commission structure?”

    The package and commission are at the bottom of this page.

02 · Five-minute role-play

Let them lead.

Give them a minute to review the package below first.

“I own a concrete company. You’re on a call Michael booked. I have good project photos, inconsistent social media and no website. I’m interested, but $3,000 sounds expensive. Show me how you’d run that conversation.”

Listen for good questions, a clear recommendation and calm objection handling. They should ask for a decision or a clear next step without guaranteeing jobs.

03 · Their questions

Hear what they want to know.

“What questions do you have for me about the offer or how we’d work together?”

04 · Wrap up

Explain the next step.

“I appreciate your time. I’ll send you a quick questionnaire. There’s space at the bottom for any other questions you have for me. Once you send it back, I’ll review it, answer your questions and let you know whether we’re a good fit to move forward. I’ll aim to get back to you within six hours of receiving it today.”

Give a different response time if six hours isn’t realistic.
